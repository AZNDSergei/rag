Ecobank is a leading pan-African banking group, founded in 1985. The full name is Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI).

Geographic presence

Headquarters: Lomé, Togo.

Operations in more than 30 African countries, including Nigeria and Ghana (key markets).

One of the largest financial institutions on the continent by geographic coverage.

Core services

Retail banking: current accounts, savings accounts, debit and credit cards.

Corporate and investment banking: trade finance, treasury, loans, advisory services.

Digital services: Ecobank Mobile App, USSD banking, online banking portals.

Remittances and international transfers within Africa and globally.

Mission & positioning

Provide accessible, convenient, and reliable banking across Africa.

Focus on financial inclusion: reaching underserved and unbanked populations.

Promote cross-border trade and financial integration within the continent.

Key features

Multilingual customer support (English, French, Portuguese in some markets).

Emphasis on digital channels (mobile banking is central to strategy).

Regulatory compliance: KYC (Know Your Customer), AML/CTF (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing).

Customer experience priorities

Fast onboarding for new accounts.

Secure document handling during account opening.

Transparent communication about processing times.

Responsive support channels: in-app chat, call centers, and branch offices.
