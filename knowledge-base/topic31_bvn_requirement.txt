Topic: 31_bvn_requirement

Example 1
User: Is BVN mandatory for opening an Ecobank account in Nigeria?
Bot: Yes. The Bank Verification Number (BVN) is required to open most accounts. If you already have BVN from another Nigerian bank, you can link it during application.

Example 2
User: What if I don’t have a BVN yet?
Bot: You may open some limited‑type accounts without a BVN but account services will be restricted. It’s strongly recommended to obtain a BVN soon.
